# Remote work

*Northwind Labs employee handbook — fictional sample document for localrag.*

Employees may work remotely up to three days a week. Tuesdays and Thursdays are office days for every team so that meetings and workshops can happen in person.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop, a monitor and a headset. A one-time home office budget of 400 EUR covers a desk chair or a desk; submit the receipt like any other expense.

## Working from abroad

Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Tell your manager and HR at least two weeks in advance, because tax and insurance rules differ between countries.

## Availability

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Keep your calendar up to date and set your chat status when you are away for more than an hour.
