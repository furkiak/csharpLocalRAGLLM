# Travel and expenses

*Northwind Labs employee handbook — fictional sample document for localrag.*

## Booking travel

Book flights and hotels through the travel portal at least 14 days before the trip. Economy class is the default for flights shorter than six hours; for longer flights, premium economy may be booked with your manager's approval.

Hotels may cost up to 160 EUR per night in most cities and up to 220 EUR per night in London, Paris, New York, Tokyo and Zurich.

## Daily allowance

The daily meal allowance is 45 EUR for domestic trips and 60 EUR for international trips. Breakfast included in the hotel rate reduces the allowance by 10 EUR.

## Filing an expense report

Submit expense reports within 30 days of the purchase. Attach a photo of every receipt above 25 EUR. Reports submitted later than 90 days are only refunded with the approval of the finance director.

Refunds are paid with the next monthly salary once your manager has approved the report.

## What is not refunded

- Minibar and room service charges beyond the daily allowance
- Fines for traffic or parking offences
- First-class train or business-class flight upgrades without written approval
