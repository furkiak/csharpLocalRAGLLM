# Information security

*Northwind Labs employee handbook — fictional sample document for localrag.*

## Laptops

Every laptop uses full-disk encryption (BitLocker on Windows, FileVault on macOS). The screen locks automatically after five minutes of inactivity. Never leave a laptop unattended in a car or a public place.

## Passwords and sign-in

Use the company password manager. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and unique for every service. Multi-factor authentication is required for email, the VPN and all cloud tools; use the authenticator app or a hardware key, not text messages.

## Reporting an incident

Report a lost device, a suspicious email or any possible data leak to security@northwind.example within one hour, even outside working hours. Call the on-call number +1 555 0100 for urgent incidents. Reporting quickly matters more than knowing every detail.

## Error codes on the badge readers

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
|------|---------|-----------|
| E-4012 | Badge expired | Ask reception for a new badge |
| E-4020 | Badge not valid for this floor | Contact your manager |
| E-5001 | Reader offline | Use the side entrance and tell facilities |

## Visitors

Visitors sign in at reception on the ground floor and receive a visitor badge. They must be accompanied at all times and return the badge when they leave.
